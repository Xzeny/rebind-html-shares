Recommendation for Rebind

Book a sponsored news post at $1,000 before banners or a giveaway. It stays on the site permanently, allows dofollow links, and sits in front of the same Windows utility-downloader audience that uses the site’s software archive.

  1. A giveaway at $1,000 is an optional sign-up add-on if a prize pool is available.
  2. Banners at these CPMs are cheap reach, but a weaker fit than a news post.
  3. If the news post is booked around Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Boxing Day, or Prime Day, the kit price is $1,500.
