Rebind vs 36 automation, peripheral, remapper, stream-control and accessibility tools · v1.2 public-safe edition, built 2026-11-15 · DRAFT / NOT LIVE

Key takeaways

  1. Where Rebind fits. Cross-OS scripting: one Luau script runs on Windows 10/11 and Apple Silicon Macs (not every API is identical). Most rivals here are Windows-first (AutoHotkey, AutoIt, PowerToys, XMBC) or macOS-first (Karabiner, Keyboard Maestro, BTT, Hammerspoon). [Verified-source c017, c149, c097]
  2. Where Rebind fits. A scripting layer that is not tied to one brand, sitting beside the vendor suites. G HUB, Synapse and iCUE program their own brand; Rebind covers standard-HID USB keyboards and mice across brands. Use both: the vendor app keeps RGB, DPI, sensors and onboard profiles. [Verified-source c153, c104]
  3. Where Rebind fits. Built-in networking, local WebSocket remote control (TypeScript/Python/Rust clients) and an MCP server for agents. BetterTouchTool (web server, CLI) and Hammerspoon are the closest rivals here, so be precise. [Verified-source c074, c079, c080, c081] [Verified-web folivora.ai]
  4. Use both: what each tool keeps. Existing AutoHotkey and Karabiner libraries stay where they are. Porting is a rewrite to Luau, and a RebindGPT draft needs Pro. Lead with use-both, never with replacement. [Verified-source c094, c095, c098, c099]
  5. Where Rebind is weaker. Platform breadth: no Linux, no Intel Mac, no mobile app yet. Karabiner (Intel), Espanso, Kanata, AutoKey, Talon, Touch Portal, Macro Deck and OpenRGB (Linux), and Touch Portal and Stream Deck Mobile (phones) cover more. Do not contrast on platform breadth. [Verified-source c016, c021, c117]
  6. Where Rebind is weaker. Software-mode limits: no mouse-movement transforms, no telling devices apart, no F13-F24 or media keys as input, and tilt-wheel scrolling never reaches scripts. XMBC, Options+ and BTT handle these mouse and trackpad jobs in software. [Verified-source c031, c033, c036, c175, c108]
  7. Where Rebind is weaker. No-code depth and adjacent jobs: Keyboard Maestro, BTT, XMBC, Pulover's and Stream Deck are GUI-first, and Rebind is script-first (recorder, marketplace, RebindGPT with Pro). Rebind is also not a voice tool, an RGB app or a firmware, so pair it with Voice Access or Talon, OpenRGB or the vendor apps, and QMK or VIA. [Verified-source c069, c139, c096, c104, c023] [Inference]

Fairness rules for writers

  1. Use only approved-claims v1.2 ids or live docs.rebind.gg pages for Rebind statements. None of the 53 Needs Dean items may be used.
  2. Rival facts come only from the official pages listed for each field (accessed 2026-11-15). If a fact has no source, it is [Unknown]: leave it out or say it is unknown. Never guess prices.
  3. Frame comparisons as 'Where Rebind fits' and 'Use both: what each tool keeps'. Never say Rebind replaces AutoHotkey, Keyboard Maestro, PowerToys, QMK, VIA or Stream Deck, and never say it 'wins'.
  4. Locked lines: 'A Stream Deck adds keys. Rebind reprograms the ones you have.' and 'Rebind does not match Keyboard Maestro's app automation; use both.'
  5. Device scope is always 'standard-HID USB keyboards and mice across brands'. Never write 'every brand', 'any device' or 'any key'.
  6. Platforms: Windows 10/11 64-bit and macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon only. For mobile, use only 'Android and iOS apps are in development'. Do not contrast on platform breadth where a rival covers more (Linux, Intel Mac, mobile, browser, onboard firmware).
  7. Never call a vendor app Windows-only unless its own page says so. G HUB, iCUE and SteelSeries GG offer macOS downloads (iCUE's Mac capability is [Unknown]; some Corsair devices use Web Hub); Synapse has a Mac Preview for macOS 15+ on Apple silicon.
  8. Hardware mode (Link/BYOH) applies to attached devices only. The built-in Mac keyboard and trackpad do not reach scripts in hardware mode.
  9. 'Up to 8,000 Hz' appears only in Link/BYOH sections, never as an evasion pitch and never as a USB polling claim. It does not belong in competitor comparisons.
  10. Do not present a rival's documented limits or project status (PowerToys reserved keys, AutoKey on Wayland) as a public weakness.
  11. Accessibility: Rebind is not a medical device. Its recipes are scripts. Make no health, condition or outcome claims, and do not tie Rebind or a rival to a medical condition.
  12. Vendor statements are verified as claims, not as proven performance. Quote them as 'X says...'.
  13. 'Positioning and hooks' and 'Paid ad evidence' come from the Paid Media review (Nov 15, 2:10-2:21 AM ET) and was not re-verified. Reddit is [Unknown] for every rival.
